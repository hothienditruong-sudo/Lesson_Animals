🎭 Role Play: At the Zoo

One student is the Zoo Keeper (ZK), the other is the Visitor (V). Ask & answer about 3–4 animals, then switch roles.

📋 Instructions:
  • Ask about appearance, size, abilities, habitat, and food
  • Use the animal table and your own ideas
  • After that, switch roles and talk about different animals
  • Finally, share your opinions: most interesting? most dangerous? like as a pet?
💬 Example Dialogue
👩 Visitor (V)
Excuse me, can you tell me about the elephants?
🧑‍💼 Zoo Keeper (ZK)
Of course! Elephants are very big animals. They have long trunks and big ears.
👩 V
Wow! What can they do with their trunks?
🧑‍💼 ZK
They can eat, drink, and carry things. They are very strong!
👩 V
Where do they live?
🧑‍💼 ZK
They live in Africa and Asia. In the wild, they live in the savannah.
👩 V
What do they eat?
🧑‍💼 ZK
They eat grass, leaves, and fruits. They eat a LOT every day!
